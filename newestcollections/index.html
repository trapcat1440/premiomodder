LSS Collection (2020)

The LSS Collection was released in 2020. The inception of our brand was introduced with LSS collection , a collection driven by ready to wear garments with our motiffs "LSS". This was the first time our garment engineers prepared hand drawn pieces until production , using this as a pathway.

Last Sinners Serving Collection (2021)

Last Sinners Serving (LSS) collection was released in 2021 as the inagural second collection. Last Sinners Serving Collection featured our first prepared to wear garments. The carrie tee was constructed by our garment engineers to fit the necessity of a classical piece of clothing for our brand.

Preludes #1 Collection (2022)

Preludes #1 collection or Preludes to Liahona was composed of only two pieces. "On target" and "LSS Dragon Shirt" were the sublte yet exclusive pieces in this collection.

The Acronym LSS Collection by PremioModder (2026)

Acronym LSS Collection was created formally by fashion house PremioModder in 2026. This collection used constructed to wear fabrics to formulate a "heavy jacket tee" one of our statement pieces.
